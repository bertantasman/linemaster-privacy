# LineMaster Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 28 October 2026

LineMaster (the “App”) is operated by FaceM Corporation (“we”, “us”). If you have a privacy question or request, contact us at facemcorporation@gmail.com.

## Information we process

### Gameplay and preferences

The App stores your scores, virtual currency, unlocked and selected cosmetic items, daily-challenge progress, and sound and haptics settings locally on your device.

### Optional gameplay analytics

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase only if you turn on **Gameplay Analytics** in the App’s Settings screen. When enabled, the App records gameplay events such as run starts and finishes, distance milestones, revives, bonus stars, power-up use, cosmetic purchases using in-game currency or rewarded ads, daily-challenge completion, screen views, and session duration. Associated event data can include distance, score, item identifier, virtual-currency amount, and app language. Firebase may also process app-instance and device information needed to provide analytics.

You can turn Gameplay Analytics off at any time from Settings. Analytics collection is disabled until you choose to enable it.

### Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob for rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, ad-viewing information, diagnostic and performance data, and device or app-scoped identifiers to provide, measure, and protect ads. The App does not ask users to create an account or directly enter a name or email address to play.

Google’s User Messaging Platform may record and apply regional advertising choices. Where required, a **Privacy Options** control appears in the App’s Settings screen so you can review or change advertising choices.

## How we use information

We use the information described above to operate and improve gameplay, provide and measure advertising, prevent ad abuse and fraud, and respect privacy choices.

## Sharing and international processing

Google provides Firebase Analytics, AdMob, and User Messaging Platform services for the App. Google may process information outside the country where you live under its applicable terms and policies.

## Your choices and rights

You can change Gameplay Analytics and applicable advertising choices from the App’s Settings screen. You can also use device-level advertising and tracking controls in iOS Settings. To request access, correction, deletion, or other rights available under applicable law, contact facemcorporation@gmail.com.

## Retention

Gameplay data stored locally remains on your device until you reset it in the App or remove the App, subject to device backup behavior. Our Google Analytics property retains event data for 2 months and user data for 14 months, with the user-data period reset by new activity. Aggregated reports and advertising-related data can be retained under Google’s applicable policies.

## Children

LineMaster is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has shared information with us in a way that requires action, contact facemcorporation@gmail.com.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The updated policy will show a new effective date on this page.
